Available Telegram Commands

/s or /search <query>

Executes hybrid search across all six formats (Anime, Manga, Novels, Korean Manhwa, Chinese Manhua) and returns ranked works with citations.

/a or /ask <question>

Synthesizes a natural language AI answer grounded strictly in verified corpus passages, explaining why each title matches.

/scope <filters>

Sets active filters for your conversation session (e.g. /scope type=manhwa,year=2023 or /scope genre=Cyberpunk).

/work <title>

Retrieves the complete unified canonical work record, cross-medium links, status, and associated chunks.

/char <name> & /va

Resolves character aliases, voice actors, and creators across Japanese, Korean, and localized romanized variants.

/stats

Displays live corpus statistics, indexed chunks count, and active vector model configuration.
