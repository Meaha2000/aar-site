You shouldn’t need to know a title to find it.

Anime Akashic Records is a semantic retrieval engine spanning 180,000+ works across anime, manga, light novels, visual novels, Korean manhwa, and Chinese manhua. Describe what you half-remember — a plot, a key character dynamic, a studio, or just the vibe and mood you’re looking for. It searches across all formats and returns ranked matches, each paired with the exact passages confirming why the work fits.

Engine Capabilities

Built for the moment when you remember a feeling, not an exact title or spelling.

Hybrid Semantic Search

Fuses dense neural vectors with BM25 lexical tokens and trigram matching into Reciprocal Rank Fusion, finished by neural reranking.

Six Formats, One Index

Anime, manga, light novels, visual novels, Korean manhwa, and Chinese manhua resolve to unified canonical works, eliminating cross-medium fragmentation.

Grounded Citations

Every ranked match includes the exact confirming synopsis excerpt or lore passage, ensuring recommendations can be verified.

Question Answering (/ask)

Conversational synthesis that produces structured AI prose answering lore questions directly, backed by catalog citations.
