The Intelligent Anime Discovery API

Find the Work You Remember. Discover the Work You Want.

Anime Akashic Records (AAR) goes beyond title lookup. Search for a work you can barely remember, or discover something new by describing the story, atmosphere, character dynamics, or themes you're looking for.

Looking for a quiet fantasy journey, a psychological thriller with morally ambiguous characters, or a story built around an unusual power? Describe what interests you in natural language, and AAR helps surface relevant works from its cross-media catalog. From forgotten titles to unexplored favorites.

What is Anime Akashic Records?

At its core, Anime Akashic Records (AAR) is a narrative, scene, and story-arc retrieval API engineered to solve the discovery problems that traditional anime databases cannot handle. While platforms like MyAnimeList, AniList, and TMDB excel at exact title lookups, broadcast schedules, and watchlists, AAR indexes the narrative itself across 144,698 canonical works, 102,574 anime episodes, 764,823 timestamped scenes (with exact HH:MM:SS ranges), and 10,576 canonical story arcs.

Pinpoint Forgotten Works & Scenes

Describe a half-remembered plot twist, a visual gag, or a single scene in plain language — AAR resolves the exact work, episode number, story arc, and HH:MM:SS timestamp window.

Discover by Premise & Atmosphere

Go beyond rigid genre checkboxes like “Action” or “Sci-Fi”. Query nuanced story premises, psychological dynamics, or up to 100 seed works with negative exclusions.

Drop-In Partner ID Bridge

Pass native IDs from 10 major platforms (mal:21, tmdb:1429, al:21, vndb:v17) directly to fetch structured episode-to-arc indexes or hybrid recommendations.

Why Choose Anime Akashic Records?

Here is what makes AAR fundamentally distinct and the premier choice for modern apps, chatbots, and recommendation systems:

1. Natural Language Search

Users and AI agents can query in everyday language. Describe a character's powers, a memorable twist, or a specific aesthetic atmosphere, and the engine connects your description to relevant titles.

2. Evidence-Grounded Results

Results include supporting text from indexed synopses helping explain why a work matches. This context helps users and AI assistants evaluate the recommendation without relying on blind guesswork.

3. Mainly Anime — Plus Connected Mediums

While focused primarily on Anime, our catalogue unifies Japanese Manga, Light Novels, Visual Novels, Korean Manhwa, and Chinese Manhua in a single index. If a hit series exists across multiple formats, it resolves cleanly.

4. Question Answering Endpoint (/ask)

A dedicated conversational endpoint tailored for AI chatbots. Ask a direct question about an anime’s setup or premise, and receive a factual, synthesized answer backed by catalog citations.

5. Flexible Hard Scope Filters

Combine the freedom of natural language descriptions with strict filters: narrow results by medium (Anime, Manhwa, Manga), status (Finished, Releasing), release year, genres, or animation studios.

6. Managed Search Infrastructure

Hosted directly on high-performance edge infrastructure (api.aarecords.tech). No need to host or maintain complex vector and retrieval clusters yourself — instant API access, managed rate limits, and ready-to-run client libraries.

7. Advanced Scene, Episode & Story Arc Engine (Anime Only)

Go beyond work-level synopses: search and recommend across 7,538 Anime, 102,574 Episodes, 764,823 Timestamped Scenes (with exact HH:MM:SS ranges), and 10,576 Story Arcs via /advanced_search, /advanced_recommend, /lore_ask, and /lore/arc-index.

8. Deterministic Retrieval vs. Live LLM Grounding

/search, /recommend, /advanced_search, and /advanced_recommend are pure deterministic database & vector retrieval engines (not conversational chatbots). Only /ask and /lore_ask invoke a live LLM to synthesize cited answers.

At a Glance: How AAR Compares

Traditional APIs and Anime Akashic Records serve complementary roles in the anime ecosystem:

Capability Traditional Anime APIs (MAL / AniList / Kitsu) Anime Akashic Records (AAR)
Primary Focus User watch-lists, episode broadcast schedules, forum profiles. Intelligent anime discovery, natural language search, narrative retrieval, and AI assistant grounding.
Search Input Exact or near-exact title keywords only. Plain natural language descriptions, plot summaries, tropes, premise details, and themes.
Tip-of-the-Tongue & Plot Description Queries Limited — requires title keywords to surface results. Built specifically to resolve descriptive memories and nuanced plot requests.
Why It Matched Returns titles and metadata IDs only. Returns supporting synopsis excerpts providing context for why the title matches.
Direct Q&A (/ask) Not available — you must build your own retrieval and grounding pipeline. Built-in endpoint delivering synthesized answers with citations.
Cross-Media Scope Often split into separate databases or IDs. Unified index: Anime, Manga, Light Novels, Visual Novels, Korean Manhwa, Chinese Manhua.
Best Used For Tracking which episode you watched yesterday. Finding what to watch next, exploring story premises, answering questions, and powering intelligent apps.

Zero ID Translation Friction

Already using MAL, AniList, or VNDB IDs in your existing application? Pass them directly to AAR (e.g. mal:9253, al:9253, vndb:v17, aar_9253). No title parsing or fuzzy matching required.

Instant Reverse Metadata Pairing

Every AAR search and recommendation response returns pre-mapped partner IDs and ready-to-use upstream URLs, allowing you to fetch live episode schedules and user reviews without secondary searches.

Who Is Anime Akashic Records For?

Streaming Services & Video Platforms

Power instant, sub-second (~500ms) search-as-you-type bars. Allow viewers to find works by describing character traits, memorable scenes, or vague plot memories without knowing exact Japanese or English titles, with strict metadata filtering for available catalog titles.

* Note: AAR surfaces candidate matches; presence in AAR does not imply streaming availability on any specific service.
AI Chatbots & Conversational RAG

Equip conversational assistants with high-density, evidence-grounded context blocks. In-memory character synergy aggregation unites plot descriptions and cast details, strictly grounding every response in verified catalog records with exact title provenance and citations — zero fabricated titles.

Autonomous AI Agents & Tool Calling

Deploy AAR as a high-precision, deterministic tool in agentic frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen). With strict OpenAPI validation, zero conversational fluff in retrieval mode, and sub-second execution, agents can query and filter catalog data with predictable latency.

Discovery & Community Bots

Build Discord and Telegram bots that answer community questions, recommend titles by plot premise or narrative tone, or automatically curate themed lists for content creators and reviewers.
